/*
 * INTERVIEW DESIGN NOTES - Food Delivery (classroom design draft)
 *
 * Implementation status: local classroom draft; requirements below describe
 * the intended design. The existing draft is preserved and is not runnable.
 *
 * Functional requirements:
 * - Browse restaurants and menus; build a cart from one restaurant.
 * - Place and pay for an order; restaurant accepts/rejects and updates preparation.
 * - Reserve an available delivery partner and track pickup/delivery.
 * - Cancel eligible orders and notify the customer of important transitions.
 *
 * Non-functional requirements:
 * - Design for idempotent payment and order submission to prevent duplicate charges.
 * - Protect order transitions and partner reservation against concurrent updates.
 * - Separate pricing, payment, delivery and notification dependencies.
 * - Preserve order price/address snapshots and make transitions auditable.
 *
 * Core entities and responsibilities:
 * - Customer/Location: identity and delivery address/location.
 * - Restaurant/MenuItem: accepting-orders flag, menu prices and availability.
 * - Cart/CartItem: selected restaurant, item IDs and quantities.
 * - Order: customer, restaurant, price/address snapshot and lifecycle status.
 * - Payment: charge/refund identity and result.
 * - DeliveryPartner/Delivery: availability, assignment, pickup and drop-off state.
 * - ZomatoFoodDelivery: orchestrates repositories and external service boundaries.
 *
 * Key operations and flow:
 * - Proposed flow: validate cart -> price snapshot -> create order -> charge with
 * - stable ID -> restaurant decision -> reserve partner -> pickup -> delivered.
 * - Define cancellation/refund policy for each state before implementing transitions.
 *
 * Design choices and invariants:
 * - Proposed: Strategy for pricing; repositories and provider interfaces for I/O.
 * - A cart must not mix restaurants; quantities and prices must be valid.
 * - Partner reservation must be atomic; delivered orders must not revert to preparing.
 * - Use integer minor units or decimal money instead of double in the final design.
 *
 * Assumptions and out of scope:
 * - This file is an unfinished pseudocode draft, not a working implementation.
 * - Payment/order states and failure compensation still need concrete design.
 * - No production routing, live GPS, real providers or distributed guarantees yet.
 *
 * Interview discussion and checks:
 * - Plan tests for unavailable items, rejected payment, duplicate requests, partner
 * - contention, invalid state changes, cancellation and refund compensation.
 * - Clarify who can cancel, when payment is captured and what happens if no rider exists.
 *
 * Run / validation:
 * - No working entry point yet; do not treat these notes as a compile guarantee.
 */
